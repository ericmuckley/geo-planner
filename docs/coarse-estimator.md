# Coarse nationwide estimator

Implemented October 22, 2026 as the first slice of the user's product reset: a simple fullstack Vercel app estimating long isochrones from arbitrary contiguous-US land origins, with refinement later. The main page defaults to this model at six hours and offers six-, ten- and twelve-hour presets. The connected provider and detailed regional experiment remain available separately.

## What the model does

The TypeScript server searches a **0.1° geographic grid** over `[-125,24,-66,50]`. Cells are about **7–11 km** across, depending on latitude and direction. This is display resolution, not a measured spatial or travel-time error bound. A land mask limits general travel to the US; lake cells are removed. Natural Earth's generalized US road lines add faster, bidirectional links between neighboring cells.

| Travel | Assumed speed |
| --- | --- |
| General land travel, including land without roads | 25 km/h |
| Major highway | 105 km/h |
| Beltway | 90 km/h |
| Bypass | 90 km/h |
| Secondary highway | 80 km/h |
| Other retained road | 55 km/h |

The current **v4** bounded Dijkstra search retains the v3 graph and v2 road costs and uses retained road geometry between the midpoints of each road’s cell visits. Each cell has one road position, chosen from its fastest/longest road visit and quantized to 0.00001°. Straight connectors to that position charge transfers between roads; times round up to 0.1 second. General land travel uses those positions at 25 km/h. V3 added geography-checked input access, mixed-cell land representatives and local shore links. V4 adds charged access along each input cell’s retained canonical-owner road visit, described below. Interior land cells without roads use their centers; new mixed shore cells use land-supported positions. This avoids free jumps between road positions, but the connectors and merged crossings remain coarse approximations. Interior overland diagonals require both adjacent orthogonal land cells; explicit shore links instead require a continuous land segment. Road-only water cells permit explicit generalized bridge alignments; they gain no unrestricted overland neighbors, only checked shore links where supported. Nearby roads and grade-separated crossings merge within cells. Speeds remain explicit assumptions, assessed on the regional sample below.

Origin validation uses the generalized US boundary and lake polygons with a **1 km boundary tolerance**. The preserved v3 rule attaches an input to its representative by continuous generalized land when possible. If that fails, checked nearby land representatives and retained-road access points provide fallback attachments at 25 km/h. Each v3 road access point follows the road visit that owns its canonical anchor, permitting explicit bridge/tunnel access without inventing a direct water crossing. V4 also considers projections/endpoints on the owning source visit in the input’s cell, paying checked local distance at 25 km/h and polyline road distance at its class speed. The minimum-time attachment per anchor seeds search; direct/v3 fallback access remains available. `attachmentKm` is the distance of the lowest-time retained seed, not necessarily the one used by every reached path. Every supported fallback seeds the search; the nearest may be an isolated land patch. An accepted input without supported access displays its own isolated cell. Shared land/traversal masks are not changed by the query. Filled MultiPolygons trace reached land cells, 495 v2 road-supported cells and 1,535 new mixed shore cells with representatives on generalized US land outside lakes, preserving holes and disconnected components. The extra display cells do not gain general travel across water; contours still fill whole cells and can extend into water or across a border. Small isolated islands can return only their local land cells for every budget. One representative per cell still merges or omits smaller land components; whole-cell shading is approximate. Ferries and international crossings are excluded; US-only source features and a coarse mask do not constitute a precise border-access model.

The endpoint accepts **60–720 whole minutes**. It returns explicit approximation warnings, model/source versions, reached-cell count, origin attachment distance and separate grid-search/contour timings. `coverageVerified` remains false. Shading does not establish an accessible road at every point. Traffic, one-way roads, turns, terrain, gates and actual access are not modeled. The regional comparisons below guide these assumptions; national travel-time accuracy remains unvalidated.

## Data and deployment

The active artifact is [coarse-grid-v4.json](../src/server/data/coarse-grid-v4.json), generated by [the v4 offline builder](../scripts/build-coarse-grid-v4.py). It retains every v3 graph/mask/anchor/shore field and speed, adding **26,946** canonical-owner road visits / **142,029** points. JSON is **7,995,142 bytes**, SHA-256 `6905fa38d198f6c89201fb731db484a1b7ff911078648c005165b5ba7be654f3`; two builds reproduce identical bytes. Only v4 is imported by the app. The generated Vercel function is **13,449,441 bytes**; local execution rejects outbound fetches. V4 remains uncommitted and has not been deployed.

Preserved comparison inputs remain unchanged: [v1](../src/server/data/coarse-grid-v1.json) (1,801,439 bytes, SHA-256 `7dfd3b30965c21a57fcca408a97a4d95e455bda33d09ecc7a29953346c23d8c4`), [v2](../src/server/data/coarse-grid-v2.json) (4,460,777 bytes, `76d3216310c8c776eab3385b077a1ff6ab85c1f72f542e9a3ff4c7fee852c063`) and [v3](../src/server/data/coarse-grid-v3.json) (4,673,811 bytes, `8cfa5616b52138c16d5a8cb8f84fd1ee4345f7e4026e88c104365cad25c523e0`). Original builders and evidence are preserved.

Sources are the roads, countries and lakes GeoJSON files in the official Natural Earth repository at tag **v5.1.2**. Source URLs and SHA-256 values are embedded in the artifact and enforced by the builder. These are **generalized cartographic data, not a current routable road inventory**. Natural Earth data are [public domain](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/about/terms-of-use/); see the [road dataset description](https://www.naturalearthdata.com/downloads/10m-cultural-vectors/roads/).

The artifact is a normal source file intended for Git and deployment. The 68.8 MB raw inputs live in ignored `data/coarse-sources/natural-earth-v5.1.2/`; the current builder needs only the pinned roads plus bundled v2/v3 inputs, never runtime source data. [Local development](local-development.md#regenerate-the-coarse-grid) gives exact downloads and build commands.

`GET/POST /api/estimate` runs inside the existing TanStack Start/Nitro app, including the generated Vercel handler. It needs no database, Python, Docker, local worker, routing-service key or network fetch. Decoded arrays are reused within a warm process; each query owns its labels and contour mask. The browser still downloads its basemap independently. Database-backed saved history is now implemented as a separate API without moving estimation into PostgreSQL or introducing PostGIS; see [saved-history setup](local-development.md#saved-history-api). The original evidence below precedes that follow-up, which is recorded in [current implementation status](implementation-status.md#saved-history-verification).

## V3 general shore connectivity

V3 applies the same geography/road rules throughout the contiguous-US grid. There are **no place names, example coordinates or regional exceptions in the production rules**. Selected trips live only in validation. Speeds remain unchanged from v2.

The builder finds land within mixed cells using the retained country/lake geometry and adds local links only between representatives on continuous land in the same country polygon component. It partitions segments at every polygon intersection, so even narrow water gaps are checked rather than skipped by sampling. Separate islands touching at a corner do not become connected. Whole-cell shading and the inherited interior overland grid remain approximate; this is not exact coastline clipping or a full access/terrain model.

Input access uses the same land check, with at most 1 km of water only at the initial input end to match shore tolerance; it cannot ignore later water gaps. Where straight land attachment fails, 245 source-derived road access points follow the actual retained road visit to its existing anchor. These let bridge-connected islands join the road network. Fallback attachment considers all supported candidates in the input cell/eight neighbors, charging each local/retained-road access distance at 25 km/h. `attachmentKm` reports the minimum initial attachment distance; different reached paths may use another supported attachment. Unsupported inputs stay isolated rather than acquiring a water shortcut. Point comparisons use these same attachments at both ends.

On the saved eleven routes, approximate point costs are unchanged (31.381m mean difference from the reference). On the **same 23 common-finite regional cases**, mean difference changes **37.730m v2 → 36.893m v3**: two improve, 21 stay unchanged, none regress. The previously unreached **24th** destination is now reachable and shaded from the opposite shore at 6h/12h. Its modeled point cost is **146.0m versus a 61.3m reference**, reported separately from common-case means. This closes a membership gap, not the missing-road/time-accuracy gap. [Comparisons, failed prototypes, tests and packaging](evidence/coarse-v3-2026-10-22/README.md).

A strict land-only attachment prototype stranded New York and Key West; a nearest-only fallback still stranded Key West on a small land patch. Both attempts are preserved. Explicit retained-road access plus multiple fallback seeds restore those origins without allowing unrelated islands to cross channels. All **42 national smoke cases** pass, with new checks against degenerate connected-origin results. Key West’s 6h cell count changes 344 → 338, while 10h/12h increase: source-access paths and display membership changed, so this is an explained model observation, not an accuracy guarantee.

Fresh validation: **39 server tests and six Python geometry tests pass**, including every 4,599 new shore link, 245 access-point land/physical bounds, unchanged road-link lower bounds, shoreline symmetry, small channels, diagonal contact, islands and bridge-origin connectivity. Check, app/diagnostic typecheck and ordinary/Vercel builds pass. The **8,464,942-byte** generated Vercel function returns representative contours with outbound fetches rejected, keeps data/credentials server-only and retains history fallback. No new environment variable, migration, database integration write test, hosted routing request, commit or deployment was needed/performed. Browser observations are in the v3 evidence.

V3 and its evidence are now committed at `887ab50`, and the user reports the current deployment looks fine. The mountain regressions from v1 → v2 remain; this release does not resolve them. National driving accuracy, long-horizon frontier accuracy and hosted v3 resources remain unvalidated.

## V4 charged road attachment

V4 retains the entire v3 graph and existing speeds. Offline source-owner recovery selects the same fastest/longest road visit used to create each pre-existing anchor. Source samples preserve bends, quantize to 0.00001° cell-relative integer offsets and include that unchanged anchor. Newly added v3 shore land representatives are not treated as road anchors. Hash checks and ownership assertions prevent attaching a visit to an unrelated representative.

For each query, new candidate access points are projections/endpoints on the input cell's owning visit. Continuous generalized land is required from input to access point, with the existing initial 1 km water tolerance. The road leg follows the retained visit's quantized polyline to its anchor, preserving explicit source-road water crossings without inventing a local crossing. Both local and road distances are charged; new times round upward to 0.1 second. Direct access and v3 fallback/bridge ports remain available. Point-arrival proxies use the same attachments, preserving symmetric model costs. No named-location or region exceptions and no speed retuning.

Both grids reach/shade every sample; no case is excluded. **The 24-case mean includes the shore destination restored by v3**, so it differs from older 23-case v2/v3 means. Both sets guide development and are not fresh holdouts, national accuracy or long-horizon contour validation. Fresh checks pass: **45 server tests, eight geometry tests, 42 national smoke cases, Biome, app/diagnostic typechecks and ordinary/Vercel builds**. National smoke cases lose no cells against v3, preserve connected origins and budget monotonicity; counts are not accuracy scores. Local browser San Diego 6h/New York 12h show v4 contours/warnings. The **13,449,441-byte** generated Vercel function passes with zero outbound requests, server-only dataset/secrets, research routing disabled and DB-free fallback.
