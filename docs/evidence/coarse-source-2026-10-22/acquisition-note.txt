The first archive acquisition used Python urllib. Its local certificate store
could not verify the Census issuer, and it stopped before downloading/writing
any source or reader file. Node's normal TLS verification succeeded for the
same official URL; no verification was disabled and no system trust changed.

Only the California Census archive and a PyPI SHA-256-verified PyShp 2.3.1 wheel
were acquired. Existing sources, artifacts, producers and evidence were not
overwritten. The wheel is imported directly from its ignored local file, not
installed into the app or globally.
